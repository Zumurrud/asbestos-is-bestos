  2. Talk 1

  3. Talk 2

    Oh! My apologies for bumping into you, my glasses fogged up suddenly… I thought I could walk back to my workbench like this, but I was carrying so many things, I had no free hand to wipe my glasses. I'll just stand here until they clear up…

  4. Talk 3

    Do I need help? No, I am fine, I know how to key data into the terminal. It's just that… Once this data is in the terminal's system, others can delete it or classify it with a press of a button. Writing it down on paper allows me to feel a little more relaxed.

  5. Talk after Promotion 1

    The polar nights of Ursus are near-endless, to the degree that even if I were to light every lamp, I would still find myself yearning desperately for the tiniest sliver of daylight, the faintest glimmer of hope. Perhaps I followed you here thinking only of proving myself more than a failure in life.

  6. Talk after Promotion 2

    Back in the Far North, I thought that by following you, I would escape that endless expanse of ice and snow. Even if I were to leave a suicide note, it would have only made for kindling. I had to go with you. You people are mad for doing what you did in Ursus. But perhaps I, too, have long fallen to madness.

  7. Talk after Trust Increase 1

    Back in Chernobog, Professor Ilia would try to cram knowledge into my brain as quickly as possible, Director Kal'tsit and the others would perform their calculations without rest, while every day I lived in fear of the punishments I would suffer for any mistake… Who would have thought those would be the best days of my life?

  8. Talk after Trust Increase 2

    'The youngest of the genius researchers'. I once held such a title with pride, but if you were to swing a pick anywhere in the Far North and break the frozen earth, you would find the Originium dust the corpses of those geniuses have become. At times, I am glad I managed to escape that place... but have I truly escaped?

  9. Talk after Trust Increase 3

    Alcohol is a fine thing. It warms the body and dulls the mind, and you need not think about whatever is happening outside... It is too warm here, and I do not rely on it to get through the day now. What should I rely on next, Doctor? Can you tell me?
