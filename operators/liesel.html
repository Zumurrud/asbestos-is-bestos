    Don't worry about me. Some problems, you can't fix right away. So long as you can get through today, you'll find a way tomorrow. So long as tomorrow comes, it means things aren't over yet. That's what I told myself after Paola's accident. Every day, many times.

  6. Talk after Promotion 2

    I'm starting to understand why Paola started the orchestra. Where you're born, what blood runs through your veins. They're obvious ties that bring a family together.   7. Talk after Trust Increase 1

  8. Talk after Trust Increase 2

    I started off helping Paola with things at home. Errands, scheduling, accounts, you know. But before I knew it, I ended up taking care of the entire orchestra, and I had to get a lot better at remembering everyone's habits. But when it comes to you, Doctor, I don't think I'll have any problems remembering, even if there were a hundred of you.
