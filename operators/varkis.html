  1. Appointed as Assistant

    This type of work is not my wheelhouse. I'll do my best, if you can explain it. What should I give you for teaching me? Well, my students bring me beans, grapes, and fins. Would any of those be appropriate?

  2. Talk 1

    Is it possible for Rhodes Island to adjust course five degrees to the west? Following this river would bring us across a floodplain. It may not affect Rhodes, but the vegetation there is fragile. The river would... no, I would thank you.

  3. Talk 2

    You can come out now, Doctor. The Medical people have left, but I'm sure they'll be back. Why are you avoiding them…? Me? Why bother wasting time treating Oripathy. Every day I still live is a gift.

  4. Talk 3

    Medjehtiqedti came looking for you. Remember to get back to her... why are you staring at me like that? I know what kind of place Rhodes Island is, and I won't do anything to the Sargonians here. But don't try telling me to 'empty the bottomless cup of hate' or whatever.

  5. Talk after Promotion 1

    If you ever get to visit Athenius, I could take you for a morning cruise on the Delphi Canal. The city is cool and quiet, with few people on the streets and few boats on the rivers. The sun rises over the temple and upon the canal, glimmering in the morning mist… I hope Minos never changes.

  6. Talk after Promotion 2

    Athenius is not my home. The Sargonians destroyed my home. The Delphi Canal is wonderful, undoubtedly. But even the real Delphi River cannot replace the memory of my river back home, the night-veiled water where the fincatching lamps sway with the current… my first river.

  7. Talk after Trust Increase 1

    —Oh! …Sorry, Doctor. Did you knock the cup over? I'll clean it up. Sorry if I spooked you, it's just the sudden sound provoked me… I'm fine. Just didn't sleep well after hearing armored burdenbeast hooves in my dreams last night.

  8. Talk after Trust Increase 2

    Where are you taking me, Doctor? This way leads to the Medical Department, and I don't think you're scheduled for an exam today. I came to repay my debt to Rhodes Island for saving my life, not to get treatment… Alright, if you insist.

  9. Talk after Trust Increase 3

    I've seen off too many young people who deserved to live longer than me… when I think about how this sort of thing could happen again, I feel… fear. But why do I see the same fear in your eyes, Doctor?

  10. Idle
