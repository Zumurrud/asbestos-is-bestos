  1. Appointed as Assistant

    A palace of memory, its structure more elaborate than a Leithanian instrument, its contents more incoherent than revenant rambling. Supporting this palace is perhaps as simple as enjoying a spoonful of soup—well, I'd say it is woven of thread rather than built of brick…

  2. Talk 1

    It's strange to perform a Deep Dive into Theresa's memory. It has lived within me since my beginning, but when I search these memories… I feel like a mere reader. It's very strange… I won't show you these Deep Dive records, Doctor.

  3. Talk 2

    Defending one's land turns a clothesmaker into a hero, and a hero into a King of Sarkaz... yes, this is where it all began: Theresis thumped on the door, and Theresa set down her scissors. Only when the door opened that evening, did she realize the heavy rain outside.

  4. Talk 3

    Only when the Goliath rushed out of the city gates that day and beheaded a Gaulish officer did we finally breathe easy. He brought back a Gaulish coat, but only handed me a piece of candy from its pocket, asking that I give it to a wounded soldier… But I couldn't do it, and I couldn't bring myself to tell him why.

  5. Talk after Promotion 1

    We took the only opportunity we had. Kal'tsit did not originally plan to kill Yliš, she merely wanted to strip the black crown from the Sarkaz, but the 'Feeble King' still had a plan to thwart hers, and thus the war dragged on… until Theresis brandished his blade.

  6. Talk after Promotion 2

    When Kal'tsit was born anew from the 'cocoon', I hesitated for a moment. But the look in her eyes was familiar. After wearing the black crown, I would occasionally see my… I thought, perhaps we didn't have to become enemies. I'm glad I considered the notion.

  7. Talk after Trust Increase 1

    Bertha wasn't young anymore then, and my mentor didn't think she'd return. She smiled as she said it didn't matter if she died in Kazdel, for her sharp-tongued disciple had no way out. We laughed over many jokes together, but this is the only one I dislike…

  8. Talk after Trust Increase 2

    I've kept this secret for so long… When the guards changed shifts, my mentor's cloth got caught on the horn of a Caprinae, causing him to trip and fall. I didn't laugh. Really. Well… Until I told Amiya about it. She'd find out eventually, after all.

  9. Talk after Trust Increase 3

    Theresis was never the type to smile. I'd poke his forehead so many times, but he never wavered. Though… on our day of victory, when we thought it was all over, when we could finally go home, I saw a smile on his face. The very next moment, that tiny crown hovered between us.

  10. Idle

    One time, I stayed awake for sixty hours straight, and Laqeramaline sang a lullaby to compel me to sleep… that was the first time I heard a Banshee sing something that wasn't an elegy, even if I only got to hear a verse and a half before I drifted off…

  11. Onboard
