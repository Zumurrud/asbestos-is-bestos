  1. Appointed as Assistant

    Your time and sanity should be left to more consequential matters. Surely you can find someone suitable to handle these trifles in your stead? Very well. For you, I shall happily serve.

  2. Talk 1

    Gnosis and Degenbrecher have taken over my Karlan Trade duties. Since this was not what I originally promised for their careers, they understandably have some complaints. There is not always a clean boundary between method and aim, but when I used this sword of mine to cleave the bridges behind me, I saw it, clear as day.

  3. Talk 2

    When I was surveying the land to find a suitable site for the observation station, I already understood the sight of it would attract numerous gazes. In this era of radical change, those timid conservatives and ignorant opportunists will meet similar fates in the dustheap. But I, SilverAsh, must ensure Kjerag is never again left behind by the times.

  4. Talk 3

    I've met with the Speaker, yes. Those of similar ideals face similar issues, perhaps even similar… kin? When Father left for Victoria, he would never have imagined a future where that nation was to be our enemy. It was indeed a simpler time, one to which we may never return.

  5. Talk after Promotion 1

    Those who seek power out of cowardice shall find nothing at the apex of authority. As for me? My friends and enemies both ensure I am unlikely to forget the importance of how I conduct myself. But I am dearly looking forward to the day I take the final step towards the peak, and find myself standing opposed to you.

  6. Talk after Promotion 2

    Kjerag seeks to build ever-greater institutions, but we shall not stand tall among the nations because of them. When we faced a true crisis, I began to believe in the strength inherent in the people of the Snow Realm. I must admit, Enya is right. It is not that I do not care for faith. I have simply long since found a belief of my own.

  7. Talk after Trust Increase 1

    There is no such thing as a chessboard without uncertainty. In a sense, every move we make is a gamble. Only when maximizing one's odds of success do one's wits come into play. Ah, the burdenbeast I bet on crossed the line first. According to custom, you are to down your glass.

  8. Talk after Trust Increase 2

    Ensia cherishes her friendship with you. Asking you to take care of her was, at the time, a somewhat unreasonable request of mine, but now I see that there could not have been a better choice. Let us not speak of the barriers between her and me. Rather than reaching any so-called understanding, I would much rather see which peak she decides to conquer next.

  9. Talk after Trust Increase 3

    Ohh? You are worried. Those in power cannot take the weight of responsibility lightly, but neither can they allow it to hold them back. At times, you need only make decisions, then entrust the rest to others. There is nothing simpler, yet more difficult. A lonely life? No, though I do look forward to having an opponent who could stand shoulder-to-shoulder with me.

  10. Idle

    …The snowfall hath relented. Let us go for a walk outside.

  11. Onboard

    It has been quite a while. The land teems with misfortune, but neither Kjerag nor Rhodes Island have fallen to the floor just yet. Are you ready? I believe we can re-establish our old rapport rather quickly. I, SilverAsh, have joined your cause as a sworn ally.
