  1. Appointed as Assistant

    All these documents need now is consolidation. I've put the references on the table, listed out the specifics, and also left some notes. Phew, the work for today and tomorrow is almost wrapped up… If you don't mind, I think I'll have a little nap…

  2. Talk 1

    Yes, I lost my sight in that one during the Bank of Mitsukue robbery. Indeed, there were two or three thousand who took part. Verily, it was an absolute bloodbath. There I was, protecting my little food cart, and with a single bamboo skewer, I killed a thousand men... Don't sweat the details. Enough about me, I'm here to hear your stories, Doctor. C'mon, out with them already.

  3. Talk 2

    Today, a student working part-time asked me about information science. I'm not much of an expert, so I shared a few stories showing how I wheedle information out of the clients at my various jobs. He did say it'd be difficult for him to include them in his university summer report, but he still looked pretty satisfied.

  4. Talk 3

    Special report! I suggest heading to the canteen early today, Doctor, something nice awaits. And remember to go to your physical later, or you'll suffer for it. Also, watch your head when passing through the upper deck. Alright, I'll take one thousand for my troubles. When did I learn divination? Why, I just happened to overhear some chatter is all.

  5. Talk after Promotion 1

    Every year, I would make the same wish at that shrine, but I'm not too sure what to wish for this year. I really envy that priest. Walking alongside the kami-sama should make life easier, right? Then again, every kami-sama's got a different personality, so if you run into one that's a real pain to work with...

  6. Talk after Promotion 2

    The whole time I was mirage-chasing, I thought I was simply experiencing another story to tell, but I ended up tying threads of fate that connected us together. With the proprietors and customers of Mitsukue, with Hoshiguma, and with you... Ahem. Anyway, I won't dismiss our friendship just because we met during a bank robbery!

  7. Talk after Trust Increase 1

    Tsk, back then, numbers like these were so monumentally oppressive, you'd throw yourself off a high-rise just to catch your breath. But in today's economy, they're gained and lost in a matter of seconds. Alright, I've decided. Doctor, I'm going to set up an education fund, to help out people like me who never finished school—Hey, wait, don't go!

  8. Talk after Trust Increase 2

    When I first set foot in the underworld, I upset some people I really shouldn't have, over some nearly worthless clues, and lost an eye for my indiscretions. Hardly a tale of fantasy and adventure, just the story of an idiot. It's ridiculous how that sort of thing turned my life around. Heh, what, you interested? Alright then. It all began one rainy night...

  9. Talk after Trust Increase 3

    I've had too many chats today. Heard too many stories, said too many words, so I'm a little tired. Oh, don't mind me. Getting to stand with you under a vermilion sky, lost in our thoughts, is rest enough for me.
