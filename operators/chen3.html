  1. Appointed as Assistant

    So, do you want a bodyguard, or a cop to watch you work 24/7? Just joking. I haven't forgotten how you do things here.

  2. Talk 1

    It's Rhodes Island's visitor logs? Swire, Hoshiguma, and Lin too… Right, assuming they're always asking about me, but there's no way they're coming this much for—Hm. I know a lot of people are waiting for me. It's not too bad, being on someone's mind.

  3. Talk 2

    Worried about my health? Guess we ought to be checking up on each other, Doctor. I've been through a lot these past few years, time sure does fly. The more you experience, the easier it is to fall into confusion and helplessness. Sometimes I do need a reminder… my time is not infinite.

  4. Talk 3

    Oh, you've noticed my blade? I was told it can erase history's mistakes, but that's practically half of whatever's been archived in the last thousand years, and there's no way a single blade could clean that all up. Let's look to the future instead—the ideals we seek still lie ahead.

  5. Talk after Promotion 1

    I followed her and Reunion to many places, and saw many sacrifices made. The people went from being lost and fragmented to becoming united and awakened—on that long and winding road. Many of them were left behind in the huge blaze in Deity Grypherburg, yet it was this very fire that ignited their hope.

  6. Talk after Promotion 2

    We're a long way from a place where everyone is treated equally, and I have no clue if I'm any nearer to this goal. But I've never doubted the equality I envision is real, for when we step up to rage against the injustice we see, we prove it exists.

  7. Talk after Trust Increase 1

    I've been back to Lungmen a few times. Other than the loss of my favorite cheongfun shop, the changes are nice to see. There are fewer L.G.D. patrols on the streets, and you hear less talk of the slums… There's no lying to myself and pretending I don't care for the city.

  8. Talk after Trust Increase 2

    I asked that man why my mother and sister had to suffer, and unsurprisingly, I didn't get the answer I was looking for. It's too bad Chi Xiao can't sever blood ties, and this writing knife can't scrape away recorded history either. There's no granting any forgiveness in their stead, no one is worth sacrificing for any 'greater good', ever.

  9. Talk after Trust Increase 3

    When things calm down, it would be nice to take you around Yan. Not just Lungmen, and not for any investigations either. Just a trip to watch the people make something from nothing in that beautiful land. There's nowhere else like it. You won't have to pack anything either. A sword and a boat. That's all we'll need.

  10. Idle

    Need a break, but can't sleep? Yeah, you have a lot of pressure on your shoulders. Sit up straight. I'll teach you some Yanese breathing exercises.

  11. Onboard

This day only comes once a year, so let's go celebrate with the others.

There's no winning our fights with brute force alone, so we have to keep improving ourselves.
