  1. Appointed as Assistant

    You want me to watch over your work, like I keep an eye on my siblings while they do their homework? Sure. I'll make you run ten laps in the snow if you don't finish on time.

  2. Talk 1

    Whoa! Head up, chest out, swing your tail—good! Treating my disease is something that deserves the greatest gratitude a hunter can give. Look how this leporibeast snorts and kicks! It's the strongest that I've hunted. I'm not sure why the medics won't accept it…

  3. Talk 2

    Apology Letter: I'm sorry, instructor. A hunter takes only what she needs. If she fails to kill with one single shot, it is not hunting, only meaningless killing. This is the rule that Kjeragandr set for the snowy mountains… and the reason I put a hole in the training target, I guess…

  4. Talk 3

    Legends tell of a creature that points the way for hunters lost in the blizzards. It is mysterious and beautiful—no, not this one on my shoulder. It got lost while following me, hoping to pick up scraps from my hunt. I call it the Rift Cloudbeast. Cool, isn't it? Wait, no, no, no, not the Doctor's hood! You can't eat that!

  5. Talk after Promotion 1

    The craftsmen know that Kjeragandr's eyes must be deep, for She sees a thousand miles. Her hands must be delicate, for She heals whatever wounds She touches. But these ideas were formed from our sculpting, one chisel at a time. At the end of the day, tomorrow depends on our own eyes and hands.

  6. Talk after Promotion 2

    When I go back, I'll tell my siblings that Oripathy isn't something to be feared. Their big sister is strong as ever and still carries her heavy crossbow around. Oripathy isn't a punishment from Kjeragandr. She guides and does not punish…

  7. Talk after Trust Increase 1

    Doctor, help me put the strand from the left side on top of the one in the middle, then do the same with the hair on the right side and weave them together—no, it's all wrong again! This is completely different from how the Saintess did it… do you actually know how to braid hair, Doctor?

  8. Talk after Trust Increase 2

    You get punished when you do something bad. The Rift Cloudbeast stole your snacks because I didn't keep a good eye on it. We'll banish ourselves, according to the rules of my house. Don't try to stop me—huh? You're saying the rule here for cloudbeasts is just three days of petting time with you? You're the master of this house, and you wouldn't lie to me. Here you go!

  9. Talk after Trust Increase 3

    You want to go hunting with me? Alright, let me first step up my training… why? There's no way you can track anything in the snowy mountains on your own. I'll need to get strong enough to carry you on my back the whole way.

  10. Idle

    Kjeragandr on high, hear my prayer… keep them safe, keep them healthy.

  11. Onboard
