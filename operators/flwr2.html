  1. Appointed as Assistant

    What might you want my help with, Doctor? Why don't you decide?

  2. Talk 1

    Here's the operational report from the Athenius branch office over the last two quarters. You'll be able to gloss what you need from the summary… Hm? You want to hear me report in detail? Now that would take quite some time. Things have been eventful for me, since my return to Minos.

  3. Talk 2

    Doctor! Visiting the Convalescent Garden? Perfect, we can catch up while we—hmm? Is that… Podenco? Did she move her entire bed into the greenhouse? *sigh* I'm barely aboard these days, Doctor. Keep an eye on her so she doesn't overwork herself, please?

  4. Talk 3

    All the new names are gradually outnumbering the old on the Garden's guestbook… When melilotus ripens, its slender pods can fire its seeds a great distance away. So too has Rhodes Island ripened… Melancholy? Me? Well, for all you know, I'm only waxing poetic as Minoans do.

  5. Talk after Promotion 1

    Going back to Minos isn't without its own headaches. Minoans all think their dreams of heroism are dashed the moment they contract Oripathy. They become totally apathetic. They hardly even bother with treatment… Do you know what? I'd have those wandering bards touring the Three Poleis, singing tales of how Infected can become heroes too!

  6. Talk after Promotion 2

    People can't help absorbing themselves in their own proficiencies, can they? That's what being a perfumer is for me, and I spent the longest time sheltering within it when I came aboard Rhodes Island. These days… Now that I know I'm made of more than that, I think it's finally, truly become mine.

  7. Talk after Trust Increase 1

    Origanon, thyme, Athos's parsley... It's back to keeping the Minoan plants I knew best of all in my seed pouch. I used to keep such a distance from these, and now they're in my hands again… These seeds haven't changed a bit, but young Lena would be shocked by just how she's grown.

  8. Talk after Trust Increase 2

    Me and… Lydia? Oh, dear, I didn't expect that name to come from you… Well, she's one of my cousins, a bit of a dependent type. If you want to know more about her, I can tell you, certainly. But if you want a story, well, I can't say the way it ends is very fun.

  9. Talk after Trust Increase 3

    Doctor, your fragrance today isn't quite the same as the usual. Did something happen? Or, did you run out of the perfume I gave you…? If it's the latter, then tell me beforehand from now on. How else will I top you off in time?

  10. Idle

    Doctor? Oh, and you're not even using lavender today. Let's see, I'll just put you on break for the next two hours… Hmm? What on Terra are all these deferred holidays for?

  11. Onboard

    I'm back, Doctor. Oh, these boronias on your desk are fresh. It seems Podenco's been minding you and the Convalescent Garden well. Anyhow, I'm back on Rhodes Island for a spell. Home for the holidays, I might say… No, no, that's a joke.

  12. Watching Battle Record

    Oh… You seem to make more sense to me now, Doctor. I used to think you were a little slapdash here and there, but now I see how almost frighteningly meticulous you are.

  13. Promotion 1

    When I first took over the Convalescent Garden, I had every intention to spend the rest of my life here, never to leave again… Whenever I intend that, fate points me in a different direction. Still, I'm starting to grow fond of how the 'unexpected' feels.

  14. Promotion 2

    My odyssey to Minos wasn't as hard as I thought it'd be. It only struck me, when I was standing in front of my family again, just how… old all those old pains had really become. Not worth dwelling on any longer. Now I'm the perfumer Lena of Rhodes Island, and that suits me just splendid.
